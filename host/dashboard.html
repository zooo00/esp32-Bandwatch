Rows keep their order while the mouse is over a table. 802.15.4 mode sweeps channels 11–26 (Zigbee usually sits on 11, 15, 20 or 25; Thread on any). Nodes are keyed by their 64‑bit extended address when a frame carried one (vendor from its OUI), otherwise by PAN/short address. Protocol is inferred from the network-layer header: Zigbee NWK, Zigbee Green Power, 6LoWPAN (Thread), or MAC-layer encryption (Thread secures at the MAC layer, Zigbee does not). Permit join means a coordinator/router is currently accepting new devices. Captures in this mode use the 802.15.4 TAP link type; Wireshark decodes Zigbee and Thread and knows the default Zigbee trust-centre key.

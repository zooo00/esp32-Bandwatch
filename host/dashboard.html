Nodes are keyed by their 64‑bit extended address when a frame carried one (vendor from its OUI), otherwise by PAN/short address. Two different measurements, one view. The single radio scans one band at a time, so each chart is as of its last scan — the status row above shows how fresh each is, with a button to re-scan it. 2.4 GHz is real RF energy (dBm) from the 802.15.4 energy detector: true power whether or not anything decodes. 5 GHz has no energy sensor on this chip, so it shows traffic activity (a 0–100 busy score from decoded Wi‑Fi) — a proxy for occupancy, not power; the two axes are not comparable. Unexplained energy flags 2.4 GHz bins with sustained power that no recently-decoded Wi‑Fi/BLE/Zigbee device accounts for — evidence of an emitter (proprietary link, video sender, RC/drone controller, microwave, interferer), not an identification.
